## SkyRoof Components

These libraries are developed as part of the SkyRoof project and are maintained in their own
repositories:

- [VE3NEA.SkySignals](https://github.com/VE3NEA/VE3NEA.SkySignals) — the `VE3NEA.Dsp`,
  `VE3NEA.SkyTlm`, `VE3NEA.SkySSTV` and `VE3NEA.SkyFM` libraries, GPL-3.0-or-later
- [ft4_coder](https://github.com/VE3NEA/ft4_coder) — AGPL-3.0-or-later; `ft4_coder.dll` is built from
  [WSJT-X](https://wsjt.sourceforge.io/wsjtx.html) sources, and the FT4 encoding and decoding it
  performs is the work of the WSJT-X developers
