SkyRoof Components

These libraries are developed as part of the SkyRoof project and are maintained in their own repositories:

  • VE3NEA.SkySignals — the VE3NEA.Dsp, VE3NEA.SkyTlm, VE3NEA.SkySSTV and VE3NEA.SkyFM libraries
  • ft4_coder — ft4_coder.dll is built from WSJT-X sources, and the FT4 encoding and decoding it performs is the work of the WSJT-X developers
